- Asset URLs are `/assets/landing/<file>`.
- The sparkle is intended for additive blending; the cloud is intended for ordinary alpha blending. Keep depth writing disabled for transparent scene sprites.
- The ring is a top-down texture with an empty center; rotate its plane into the arena floor.
- Contact sheet (local review artifact, not committed): `frontend/.screenshots/x1/contact-sheet.png`.
- Full-body sprites use a 512 × 768 canvas with feet near bottom center; align billboards by their bottom center. Portraits are 256 × 256 with transparent bust silhouettes.
